Atopic Dermatitis (Eczema)

Diagnostic Confidence 94.6%

Condition Overview

Atopic dermatitis is a chronic inflammatory skin condition causing itchy, red, swollen, and cracked skin. Common in flexural areas like elbows and knees.

Personalized Healthcare Guidance

  • Apply fragrance-free moisturizing creams twice daily after bathing.
  • Avoid harsh synthetic soaps, hot water showers, and scratchy fabrics.
  • Use topical hydrocortisone ointment as advised by a qualified specialist.
Recommended Do's
  • Keep skin hydrated with ointment
  • Use mild, gentle cleansers
Avoid Don'ts
  • Do not scratch inflamed patches
  • Avoid direct sunlight exposure
